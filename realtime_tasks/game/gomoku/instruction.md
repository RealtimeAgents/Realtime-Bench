A real Gomoku (五子棋, five-in-a-row) game is open in the browser, ALREADY configured to
play against the computer AI on EASY, with YOU as BLACK (you move first). Do NOT touch
any control in the right-hand panel (mode / difficulty / side / Forbidden moves) — they
are already set correctly. Just click empty intersections on the 15x15 board to place your black stones;
the AI (White) replies after each move. Connect FIVE of your stones in a row (horizontal,
vertical, or diagonal) before the AI does, and block the AI when it lines up four.

You have 10 seconds for each move. The "Move clock" in the top bar counts down whenever it
is your turn — including while you are thinking — and resets after you place a stone; if it
reaches 0 you lose on time. Keep playing until the game is decided (you connect five, the AI
does, or your clock runs out). There is no stop action; just keep playing.
